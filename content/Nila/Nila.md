#### *The Moonlit Grove*

**Population**: 20,000

Nila is a grove-settlement of woodland paths, orchards, clearings, still water, garden halls,
moonlit gatherings, reflection pools, old customs, and quiet beauty. It feels less like a dense
town and more like a community woven through the living landscape. Paths matter here. So do
pauses, rituals, songs, seasonal changes, and the difference between looking at something and
truly attending to it.

Eladrin and Firbolgs are especially common in Nila. Eladrin influence can be seen in the
settlement’s artistry, emotional expressiveness, seasonal customs, and love of beauty. Firbolg
influence can be felt in its patience, rootedness, gentleness, and care for the living world.

Together, they give Nila a culture that values reflection, harmony, memory, and beauty, lived
rather than displayed for status.

Visitors often describe Nila as peaceful, but that does not mean simple. Nila’s people may be
gracious, but they are attentive. They tend to notice haste, arrogance, grief, hidden anger, and
carelessness. Many of the settlement’s customs encourage people to slow down enough to
understand what they are actually feeling before they act.
