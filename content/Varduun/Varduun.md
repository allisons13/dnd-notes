#### *The Stonebound Hold*

**Population**: ???

Varduun lies among rocky uplands where quarrying, stonework, metalwork, and durable craft shape daily life. It is a settlement of hard ground, forge heat, steep paths, carved stone, old excavations, mule tracks, workshops, terraces, and tools made to last. The people of Varduun are known for building things that endure.

Dwarves and Goliaths are especially common here, though Varduun is not exclusive to any one people. Dwarven craft traditions and Goliath endurance have strongly shaped the town’s reputation. The culture prizes competence, proof, and practical excellence. A claim matters less than the work behind it. A promise matters less than whether it holds under pressure.

Varduun can seem severe to outsiders, but it is not joyless. Its pride is rooted in skill, resilience, and the satisfaction of making something solid from stubborn material. It is also known for nearby hot springs, where workers, travelers, and locals gather for rest, conversation, recovery, and argument in equal measure.
