***From Session 3:*** *The [[Tally Offices]] are where we are most likely to find [[Alaric “Larry”|Larry]] and after arriving in town we head straight there. We meet Larry in his offices and he is very busy and is headed to another meeting, so he tells us to meet him here tomorrow. As we are leaving Larry's office a young woman runs by very quickly, knocking Sasha over. When Sasha gets up she realizes that she is very familiar with the woman.*  

It's [[Eadaz (Ead) Nara|Ead]], [[Sasha - Aleksandra Dmitrievna Mirova|Sasha]]'s girlfriend... who she has yet to mention... Ead's a little surprised to see Sasha back so fast, after their difficult departure. Ead says that she has found something on the farm that could mean that Sasha was right, that maybe it was arsonists that set fire to her family farm. 

Ead shows the group a cracked clay pot, she found it in a drainage bank that the recent rain has exposed, the inside of the pot is scorched black, there is a odd green wire attached to it, it seems that the pot was placed deliberately in the area to set fire to the farm. Ead was walking in the area while it was raining, she tends to do that as of late, and stumbled upon the pot. The pot itself isn't strange they are typically used for controlled burns of different sorts, they are super common. The green wire is not typical of these kinds of pots, which has given Ead pause. She recommends going to find [[Mooh Gill]] a toolsmith, he repairs everything, he is our best shot of figuring out what is going on with this. 

[[Mooh Gill's Workshop]] - its lowkey a mess, he's a nice guy - he says that the wire is not [[Halenrow]] make, that its salt wire from [[Tidera]], the wire does better in that area, it doesn't rust like the regular iron typically used in this area. The wire is non-functional to the pot, so it's odd that it is on the pot, it makes it distinctly not from Halenrow. The burn in the pot is typical, nothing out of the ordinary. Sasha tells Mooh that she thinks that the pot was used to set fire to her family farm, Mooh says that the last person he saw have this wire was Ead's father. 

[[Nara Family Farm]], the barn is painfully ordinary, [[Shyrrik Zentharis|Shyrrik]] sees a small coil of salt wire tucked away in a corner of a shelf, the coiling, coloring, and age all seem to match the wire that is on the pot. There is no other wire around, this is the only wire that is being used on the farm. After finding the wire, the group goes to find Ead's father, [[Cassar Nara]], he might be in the [[Tally Offices]]. The clerk does not know where Cassar is, he instead hands over files regarding Sasha's family farm, they find files showing that Sasha's family had tried to buy a plot of land separate from the family farming collective. The purchase was voided most recently because her parents died, but before it was objected and stopped by the Nara family. 

Back at [[Nara Family Farm]], the gang presses with all evidence and Cassar admits what he has done... he accidentally killed them, he just wanted to set fire to the farm to stop them from buying more land and leaving the collective.   

Cassar turns himself in at the Tally Offices, and we get to actually asking [[Alaric “Larry”|Larry]] about the key, he doesn't know about a key or anything stored in a vault, but he recommends looking into records, trying to find things that Halenrow has tried to forget. He pulls out a bunch of records and maps, we see that there was once a communal well, that is now covered up by the Tally Offices, the well might still exist in the ground floor of the offices. [[Blewit]] finds a spot that is "giving" and [[Shyrrik Zentharis|Shyrrik]] with his amazing #Plumbing skills thinks there might be a well there, Sasha with her #Carpentry skills peels up the floor boards and there is in fact a well! [[Tumdrill Stoneblessed|Tumdrill]] goes down into the well first, he sees a light down at the end of the hallway in this well. Larry is fucking buff so he helps us for a bit by holding onto the rope as we descend down into the old well, but he's not gonna hang around, so he ties the rope off for us cause he has more important shit to do.  

Down the hall in the [[Ancient Abandoned Well]], Tumdrill sees an odd blue light, Blewit casts #Detect_Magic to identify what the light might be - he detects **all** forms of magic in the room...

We enter a large, tranquil chamber bathed in blue light. In the floor there are eight pools of water. On the surface of each pool there is a reflection of the room above. High above is a vaulted ceiling with a beautiful painted mural depicting an angel is surrounded by eight doors. She holds a scroll in her right hand on which you can read the words: 

> [!quote] 
> “Look at me and I look at you. I never speak a word but I always tell the truth.”

There appears to be no way out from this room. However, looking deep into the water, you see a door at the bottom of each pool.

While Blewit takes a look into the 7th pool, associated with Necromancy, the angel from the mural looks extremely old, ancient. The angel moves and attacks! 

The pools under the mural, the schools of magic they are associated with, and what they reflect: 
>Pool #1: Divination - weeping
>Pool #2: Conjuration - the snakes in the mural slowly begin to move
>Pool #3: Enchantment - no change, swirling, the water is moving 
>Pool #4: Illusion - you see yourself with everything you've ever wanted, no mural in the pool 
>Pool #5: Abjuration - no change, still water
>Pool #6: Transmutation - no change except the room has a greenish hue
>Pool #7: Necrotic - the angel is old as hell 
>Pool #8: Evocation - no change, still water
