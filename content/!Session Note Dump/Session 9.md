### INTO THE MOUNTAIN
Our first stop is the [[Stone Singer Hall]], its a like if library met a dojo, its very quiet and we are greeted by some monks who are reading, Tumdrill asks after his mother, [[Dís Stoneblessed]], who just left for home not too long ago. We head over to Tumdrill's parents home, we get a bit more backstory from Tumdrill and his father's pacifism and what we can and cannot say to him. [[Thorin Stoneblessed]] is at the door, he doesn't seem too surprised that Tumdrill is here, but is surprised that the rest of the party is here, he heard of the confrontation with the Ankhegs. His father is disappointed with the number of weapons the party has on them. 

Tumdrill asks about the [[Stonebound Accord]] and the [[Lowerworks]] - his father says that the Lowerworks are dangerous and the accords were signed for a reason. The were signed to protect the people of [[Varduun]] they are under shared Civic and Stone Singer custody. 

The Ankhegs were disrupted by tremors, [[Weckdrill]] and the Stone Singers are looking into this more but Tumdrill's father doesn't know how these events are connected or what those things have to do with the founding of [[Velmora]]. 

[[Sasha - Aleksandra Dmitrievna Mirova|Sasha]] talks to Dís, she asks about the Stone Singers and the [[Lowerworks]], Dís says that the Lowerworks used to be open to the public, you would need special permission to access them - the [[Stone Singers]] have access.
