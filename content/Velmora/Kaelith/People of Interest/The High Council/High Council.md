The High Council is composed of representatives from Velmora’s major settlements. These representatives are chosen according to local custom and gather in [[Kaelith]] in the [[Council Ward]]to address island-wide concerns. 

High Council Reps spend most of their time in their respective hometowns - coming only to Kaelith for High Council meetings
