Located in: [[Kaelith]]

*The most prestigious magical institution on Velmora. The College is not only a place for wizards and scholars; it influences public life, civic planning, education, religious observance, and the island’s shared respect for knowledge. Public lectures, magical demonstrations, copied treatises, student debates, and shrines to Kaenum all help shape the city’s atmosphere.*

The [[Archmage Liora Vael]] has offices here. It is also the location of [[The Archives]].
