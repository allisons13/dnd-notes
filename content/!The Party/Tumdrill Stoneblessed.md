#### *Dwarf Monk from [[Varduun]]*

#### BACKSTORY
>*Tumdrill Stoneblessed was born in the rumbling depths of Varduun, a hardy dwarven mining town carved into the roots of the Hearthstone Mountains.  From a young age, he has been caught between two powerful and conflicting legacies.*
>
>*His mother is a respected Stone Singer, part of an ancient lineage of dwarves who treat the mountain not merely as stone, but as a living, breathing entity. Stone Singers spend years in deep meditation learning to read the subtle vibrations traveling through rock — developing a keen tremor-sense that warns of danger, collapse, or intruders. From this awareness they created the Kata of the Living Stone, a graceful yet powerful martial art focused on redirection, precision, and explosive force when needed. Above all, Stone Singers believe their abilities exist to protect their loved ones and those who cannot protect themselves.*
>
>*His father, once a capable warrior and mine guard, turned away from all violence after a terrible tragedy.  He doesn’t speak of it, but whatever happened, the guilt shattered him. He laid down his arms that day, swore a solemn vow to do no harm, and dedicated himself to the healing arts. He is now a dedicated physician and an unwavering pacifist who believes all violence, even in self-defense, only breeds more suffering. He has strongly encouraged Tumdrill to abandon his mother’s martial traditions and follow him into medicine, hoping his son will become a healer rather than a fighter.*
>
>*"There are two kinds of people in this world, son. Those who save lives. And those who take lives ... . You can't protect by killing."*
>
>*Tumdrill inherited the sturdy build of his people, but also an unusual gift: exceptional speed and agility rarely seen in dwarves. Growing up sparring and racing with goliath peers, he learned early how to compensate for his smaller stature with superior dexterity, lightning reflexes, and acrobatic movement. These talents blended seamlessly with the Stone Singer training he received in secret from his mother, allowing him to move with fluid grace and surprising power.*
>
>*Now in his early adulthood, Tumdrill lives in the bustling capital of Kaelith, where he is enrolled in medical school at his father’s insistence - Physicians College, just outside the College of Arcana. He excels in his studies and truly enjoys helping the sick and injured. Yet he cannot ignore the call of the mountain and the martial rhythm that flows through his blood.*
>
>*Deeply torn, Tumdrill struggles to find balance. Part of him longs to fully embrace the Kata of the Living Stone and become the protector his mother’s lineage expects. Another part fears that choosing violence — no matter how righteous the cause — would betray his father’s painful lesson and the vow of peace he holds dear. He now searches for a path where his considerable talents can serve good without violating the sanctity of life.*
