#### *The Northern Port*

**Population**: ???

Tidera is Velmora’s northern port: a town of docks, salt wind, rope, smokehouses, fishing boats, shipwrights, wet stone, and practical people who understand that survival often depends on maintenance done before anyone applauds it. Life here follows the rhythm of tides, storms, work crews, repairs, preservation yards, and harbor schedules.

Tidera is not known for elegance or pretense. It respects usefulness. People who can mend a sail, secure a mooring, preserve a catch, read bad weather, settle a dockside argument, or notice rot in a beam before it fails are valued here. Dragonborn and kobolds are especially common in Tidera, though the town is home to many peoples. Kobolds are vital to the working life of the harbor, and Dragonborn are often looked to as protectors, elders, or visible figures of responsibility.
