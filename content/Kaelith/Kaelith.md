#### *The Capital*

**Population**: 75,000
Kaelith is the largest city on [[Velmora]] and the island’s political, scholarly, and magical heart. It sits near the island’s central eastern inlet, close enough to benefit from trade, travel, and civic movement without being defined only as a port. People from every region eventually pass through Kaelith: students, petitioners, merchants, laborers, scribes, minor officials, pilgrims, apprentices, and travelers with business before the Council or the College.

The city is home to the [[College of Arcana]], the most prestigious magical institution on Velmora. The College is not only a place for wizards and scholars; it influences public life, civic planning, education, religious observance, and the island’s shared respect for knowledge. Public lectures, magical demonstrations, copied treatises, student debates, and shrines to Kaenum all help shape the city’s atmosphere.

Kaelith is also the seat of the [[High Council]]. Representatives from the other four major settlements gather here to address island-wide concerns, negotiate the use of shared resources, and preserve cooperation between regions. Kaelith itself serves as host and administrative center rather than sending a separate voting representative, though the [[Archmage Liora Vael|Archmage]] is an unofficial member and sits in on all High Council business.
