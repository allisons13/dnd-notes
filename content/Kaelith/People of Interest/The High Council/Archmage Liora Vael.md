*Archmage Liora Vael is the most respected magical authority on [[Velmora]]. She is associated with the [[College of Arcana]] in [[Kaelith]] and is widely known for her wisdom, discipline, power, and long service to the island.*

*Liora appears far younger than her reputation suggests. Velmorans generally understand that she is much older than she looks, though the exact details are not common knowledge. This is usually treated as another sign of her unusual magical nature rather than a scandal or mystery.*

*To most people, Liora represents continuity, seriousness, and protection. She is not a formal ruler of Velmora, but her voice carries weight, especially in matters of magic, scholarship, history, and the College.*
