#### *The Breadbasket*

**Population**: 58,000

Halenrow is the agricultural heart of Velmora. Much of the island’s food begins here: grain, fruit, vegetables, livestock, orchard goods, and the steady labor that keeps everyone else from having to think too hard about where dinner comes from. It is a place of fields, mills, barns, orchards, market carts, grazing land, and weather-worn roads.

Halenrow is not flashy, but it is essential. Its people tend to value reliability over drama and usefulness over ornament. Farmers, millers, drovers, orchard keepers, granary workers, and seasonal laborers all contribute to the region’s identity. A person’s reputation here is often built through consistency: showing up, doing the work, helping when harvest turns difficult, and not making simple things harder than they need to be.

The town has a grounded warmth, but not a sentimental one. People may welcome strangers, but they still notice whether those strangers respect work, tools, land, animals, and time. Halenrow’s importance to Velmora gives it a quiet authority. When the fields suffer, the whole island eventually feels it.
